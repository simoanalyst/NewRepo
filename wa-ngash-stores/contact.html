Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Nairobi deliveries are typically completed within 24–48 hours. For other counties, expect 2–4 business days. Same-day delivery is available in Nairobi CBD for orders placed before noon.
What is your return policy?
We offer hassle-free returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached. Contact us via WhatsApp or email to initiate a return.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa (Till number & Paybill), Visa/Mastercard credit and debit cards, and Cash on Delivery for Nairobi and major towns within Kenya.
Do you offer size exchanges?
Yes! If the size doesn't fit, we'll happily exchange it within 30 days. We recommend consulting our Size Guide before ordering to find the perfect fit. Exchanges are free for the first request.
Are all products authentic?
Absolutely. Every product at Wa-ng'ash Stores is 100% authentic and sourced directly from verified manufacturers and distributors. We have a strict quality verification process before any product enters our catalogue.
Do you ship outside Kenya?
Currently we ship to all 47 counties in Kenya. International shipping to Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda is available on request. Contact us directly for international order inquiries.
