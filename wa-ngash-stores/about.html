How It Began

Crafting Style Since 2015

Wa-ng'ash Stores was born in 2015 from a simple belief: every Kenyan deserves access to premium, stylish fashion accessories without having to travel to the malls or pay imported prices.

Founded by Margaret Wanjiku in a small shop along Kenyatta Avenue, the store started with just 20 pairs of shoes and a dream. Word spread fast. Within six months, the shop had expanded into eyeglasses and handbags — and a loyal following was growing.

Today, Wa-ng'ash Stores serves thousands of customers across Kenya, with a curated catalogue of over 500 products, same-week delivery to major towns, and a reputation for quality that keeps customers coming back.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To make premium fashion accessories accessible to every Kenyan, delivering exceptional quality, honest pricing, and a shopping experience that makes you feel valued — from browsing to delivery.

We believe style should not be a privilege. It should be a right.

🌟

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted and beloved fashion accessories brand — known for quality that lasts, styles that inspire, and a community that celebrates self-expression.

We see a future where Wa-ng'ash Stores is a household name across the continent.

Who We Are

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision we make, every product we select, and every customer interaction.

Quality First

Every product is carefully vetted for craftsmanship, durability, and aesthetic appeal before it enters our catalogue.

Customer Love

Our customers are the heart of everything we do. Their satisfaction is not just a metric — it's our daily motivation.

Integrity

We are transparent about pricing, honest about product descriptions, and fair in all business dealings.

Style Excellence

We stay ahead of trends without chasing fads, curating timeless pieces that look as good in five years as they do today.

Community

We are proud to be a Kenyan business serving Kenyan communities, and we give back through local partnerships and programs.

Sustainability

We prioritize sustainable sourcing and eco-friendly packaging to minimize our environmental footprint.
